How it works

Natural mode uses the analytical slope of a radial height wave to approximate refraction. A weaker trailing packet, distance damping and independent fade produce a softer wake. Edge protection reduces stretched borders.

This is a screen-space approximation, not a fluid solver. Images stay in your browser.

Reference: NVIDIA GPU Gems Chapter 1

Ripple settings

LINEAR = steady speed. OUT = fast start, slow finish.

Export settings

Natural mode needs natural.frag and the additional uniforms. Exported settings are not drop-in options for the old game service.
